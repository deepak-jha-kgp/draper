The 6:14 to Carrickfergus now runs every twenty minutes.

From 3 November the coastal line moves to a turn-up-and-go timetable. No booking, no seat reservation, and the last train back leaves an hour later than it does today.

Every 20 minutes

06:14 to 23:40, seven days. The old pattern held gaps of fifty minutes on Sundays; those are gone.

One fare, any train

Off-peak pricing all day. A single is £4.80 whichever service you board, and returns stay valid for a month.

Later last train

23:40 out of Central, up from 22:35. The 00:10 relief runs on Fridays and Saturdays through the winter.

You no longer need a timetable for this line. Turn up at the platform and a train will be along inside twenty minutes.
